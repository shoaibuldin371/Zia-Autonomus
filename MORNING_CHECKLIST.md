# Operator Morning Checklist (5-Minute Routine)
*Operator: Zia Ul Din (Karachi, PKT UTC+5)*

Follow this 5-minute checklist daily at 09:15 PKT following the morning automated discovery run:

---

### Step 1: Health & Calm Status Check (30 seconds)
- [ ] Open the **Today** dashboard (`/`).
- [ ] Check the status chip: confirm it shows **"All systems calm"** (green indicator).
- [ ] Verify that Emergency Stop is inactive and database connection reports healthy.

### Step 2: Triage Hot Replies (1 minute)
- [ ] Check the **Hot Replies** counter on Today.
- [ ] If any replies are flagged (e.g. asking for pricing, timeline, or meeting preview), open them immediately and respond.

### Step 3: Review Drafts in Emails Queue (2 minutes)
- [ ] Navigate to **Emails** (`/emails`).
- [ ] Review the morning drafts generated by the 09:00 PKT scheduled discovery.
- [ ] Click **Approve & Dispatch** for qualified drafts; click **Reject** for any that do not fit.
- [ ] Confirm that your daily send count remains within your configured speed limit (e.g. 25/day).

### Step 4: Check Manual Contact Leads (1 minute)
- [ ] Navigate to **Manual** (`/contact-manually`).
- [ ] Check priority phone/WhatsApp leads with missing websites.
- [ ] Place calls or send personalized WhatsApp preview scripts using the 30-Second Script template.
- [ ] Click **Mark Contacted** to enforce the 72-hour cross-channel gap.

### Step 5: Check Hot Client Requests Desk (30 seconds)
- [ ] Navigate to **Hot Client Requests** (`/requests`).
- [ ] Paste any new project postings from Upwork or remote client boards.
- [ ] Review the classified proposal and copy to clipboard for on-platform submission.
